# Company profiles

Research this company produces a source-backed business profile and operating-stream proposal. The identity header shows the supplied company name, research summary, industry, website, team-size and market findings. A monogram is a placeholder, not an extracted logo. Social links appear only when exact supported-platform URLs occur in retrieved official-site text; missing links are omitted. Source presence is not independent account verification.

The latest completed brief remains available even after five newer failed jobs. If the form changes, the prior profile is visibly identified as saved research; it is not relabeled with the new inputs. Older operating-only classifications have an explicit research callout.

After confirming streams, Update after kickoff saves a revised summary, industry and advisor corrections. Record who confirmed the corrected facts and when. The advisor-only review API checks company access, source-job ownership, and company revision, then audits the prior/current review. It does not rewrite public findings or turn them into verified claims. Matching kickoff context includes the corrections. A subsequent research job leaves the previous review stored but does not apply it to the new draft.

Download company profile exports a self-contained HTML deliverable with dates, research qualifications, advisor updates, original findings, quotes, source links, and saved primary/supporting operating streams. Open in a browser to print or save as PDF. No scripts or external assets are required; all text is escaped.

Comparable companies need evidence of overlapping offers and buyers. Size similarity requires evidence for both companies; otherwise the model must state comparison is unconfirmed. A stronger prompt does not guarantee that public sources disclose headcount or useful peers. Real-provider research quality remains an acceptance check. Automatic logo/branding extraction is deferred.

Save & continue to send persists the reviewed kickoff request and opens Preview & send kickoff request. Send invitation email is the actual delivery action and requires configured Resend/sender settings. No email is sent by saving a profile or request.

The shared snapshot shows concise fact bullets and equally sized value streams. Potential competitors link to matching saved company sources when available. Expand “Research details & sources” for original wording, qualifications and citations. Later Discovery steps focus on their current task rather than repeating the stream diagram.
