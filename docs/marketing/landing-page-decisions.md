# Landing page design decisions

## Confirmed by the owner, September 5, 2026

- The company/workflow graph is a marquee product feature.
- Place a large, authentic app screenshot in the first section immediately below the hero, rather than a generic diagram.
- Use the approved DutyGraph mark and the existing charcoal/ivory app style.
- Show a coherent named workflow with readable task cards, handoffs and a plainly labeled issue under investigation.
- Use fictional Cobalt data and label it as illustrative. Exclude account details and operational secrets.
- A supporting detail image can show a clicked handoff with its receiving conditions and human accountability.

## Gallery and feature sections

The owner requested a two- or three-screen automatic gallery immediately below the fold. Use three authentic, illustrative screens with a short explanation for each:

1. **See how work moves.** Supplier onboarding, with its normal path, exceptions and unresolved approval responsibility.
2. **Give every task a clear job.** The task library: Human, AI and AI + human review, organized by value stage.
3. **Know what happens next.** A task detail or receiving handoff showing the work product, software, human checkpoint and supporting evidence.

Advance slowly (about eight seconds); provide previous/next buttons, pagination and a pause control. Pause on hover, keyboard focus and manual interaction. Respect reduced-motion preferences and never move keyboard focus automatically. Captions must remain available without animation.

Further feature sections should explain task cards as living work instructions, evidence and participant corrections, and human accountability for proposed agents. Avoid implying every software logo is an active connector or that proposed agents already run. Draft copy and screenshot selection remain open for owner feedback.
