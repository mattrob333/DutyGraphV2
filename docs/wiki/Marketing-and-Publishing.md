# Marketing and publishing

## Positioning

The website should recruit pilot companies and help advisors understand the discovery outcome. Lead with understanding work and finding useful improvements. Governance is the downstream reason to keep duties and authority connected.

Do not market unverified leadership in the category, guaranteed speed, established partnerships, or automatic agents. Asynchronous discovery can reduce scheduling friction; the magnitude must be measured in pilots.

## Lead capture

Public intake is stored separately from company evidence. The operator inbox is private. Notifications require a configured/verified sender; a saved lead is not proof that an email arrived or a pilot was accepted.

Use the private operator tooling described in [pilot discovery](../pilot-discovery.md). Never paste inbox output into public issues. The public inquiries address is hello@dutygraph.com; forwarding configuration is operational, not proof of outbound email capability.

## Publishing checklist

- Edit source content/generators and regenerate.
- Check claims, citations, images, canonical URLs, and schema.
- Check desktop/mobile layout and actual CTA destination.
- Keep demo, form submission, and notification claims separate.
- Verify hosted output after publication.
- Preserve evidence dates when directory content changes.

See [SEO strategy](../seo-strategy.md) and [newsletter operations](../newsletter-operations.md). Analytics configuration and real conversion measurement must be verified separately; content generation alone does not establish attribution.
