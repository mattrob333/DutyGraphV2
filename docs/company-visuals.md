# Company graph and roster onboarding

Request a people roster before or just after the leadership kickoff. Discovery and Company graph both offer **Import people CSV**, with a downloadable template. Include name, email, title, department, and manager_email. The advisor uploads the client-provided file, previews validation and the chart, then imports valid rows. Invalid rows remain flagged for correction. This is not a client file-upload portal.

Company graph opens to the reporting chart. Pan, zoom, fit the chart, or use the minimap. Select a person to explore their recorded duties and tasks. Reporting lines come from the roster; importing them does not independently confirm them. Teams & duties and By team remain available.

Open **3D explorer** for the connected node network. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, use department or control scenario filters, and select a node to highlight its neighbors. Search provides a selectable record list. Larger nodes have more connections, not higher risk. Isolated records remain available through the other company views. The explorer is bounded to 150 source nodes and reports partial results when applicable.

The engine label identifies Neo4j Aura or the current-record fallback. Department and software nodes are derived from recorded fields; they do not establish access permissions. Control mappings identify work for review, not proof of compliance. Browsers without WebGL can use the record list.
