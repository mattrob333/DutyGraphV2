# Company graph and roster onboarding

Request a people roster before or just after the leadership kickoff. Discovery and Company graph both offer **Import people CSV**, with a downloadable template. Include name, email, title, department, and manager_email. The advisor uploads the client-provided file, previews validation and the chart, then imports valid rows. Invalid rows remain flagged for correction. This is not a client file-upload portal.

Company graph opens to the reporting chart. Pan, zoom, fit the chart, or use the minimap. Select a person to explore their recorded duties and tasks. Reporting lines come from the roster; importing them does not independently confirm them. Teams & duties and By team remain available.

Open **Relationships** for a guided investigation. Choose control-related agents, a person, tasks without a recorded owner, or tasks with a proposed AI operating mode. Select a result to see labeled directional connections; click a neighbor to continue exploring. The diagram shows up to four immediate neighbors to keep labels readable. The expandable connection list includes the remaining direct relationships. Review record opens the underlying details and evidence.

Software and control mappings are summarized from the tasks shown. They do not establish access permissions or compliance. The engine label identifies Neo4j Aura or the current-record fallback; partial graph results are explicitly identified. Empty results do not establish absence of risk. No new AI analysis is performed by these filters.
