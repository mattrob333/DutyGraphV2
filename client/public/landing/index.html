    Research the business.

    Public research → Meeting preparation
  2. 02

    Hear from the leaders.

    Capture goals, departments, and responsibilities. Turn the kickoff into a proposed team roster you can review.

    Leadership input → People & duties
  3. 03

    Ask the people who do the work.

    Send individual question sets through private response links. Bring the team's accounts into the evidence record.

    Team responses → Draft task cards
  4. 04

    Confirm. Connect. Improve.

    Review task descriptions with their people. Explore handoffs, test possible constraints, and define where AI could help.

    Confirmed work → A plan to improve

A few useful answers

Before you
bring the team.

Who is DutyGraph for?+

Advisors, business leaders, and technology teams who need to understand how a company works before changing it. Start with one company or one area of work, then build out the record.

Can I explore without inviting anyone?+

Yes. Create an account and open your private fictional Cobalt sample company. It includes people, duties, task cards, and example workflows. Nothing is sent to anyone when you open the sample.

Does DutyGraph run my AI agents?+

The current pilot helps you define and review proposed AI work, tools, and human checkpoints. It does not run agents inside your business systems. A software logo on a task describes the tool used; it does not mean that tool is connected.

What do I need for research, AI, and email?+

Add your provider keys in Workspace settings: Exa for research, OpenAI for AI drafts, and Resend for invitation emails. Email also needs a verified sender. Provider usage is billed by those providers. You can explore the sample and maintain records before connecting them.

How do you distinguish facts from AI suggestions?+

AI output is a draft. Sources remain attached to the work, uncertain claims remain visible, and people review and confirm task versions. A proposed duty or a possible constraint is not silently treated as an established fact.

What will a team member receive?+

An invitation with a private link to their questions. They can describe their work in their own words. Their response comes back to the advisor for review and becomes part of the engagement's evidence.
