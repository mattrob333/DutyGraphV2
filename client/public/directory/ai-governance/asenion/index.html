Asenion

AI governance, risk and security platform formed when Canada's Fairly AI acquired Sweden's anch.AI in June 2025 and rebranded as Asenion. It markets automated controls that continuously assess and test AI systems and agents against frameworks including the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF.

Vendor claim

Company site states Asenion provides automated controls that continuously assess, test and govern AI systems and agents across the lifecycle, with verifiable, tamper-resistant assurance and coverage of regulations and standards including the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF.

Limit: Marketing page; test methods, what makes records tamper-resistant, and framework coverage detail are not evidenced, and no registry/approval features are described.

Vendor claim

A company post dated June 18, 2025 states Fairly AI acquired Swedish start-up anch.AI to launch Asenion as the new company name, covering predictive, generative and agentic AI, with testing agents for fairness, privacy and security, and that the platform has been made available.

Limit: Vendor announcement; deal terms and current product continuity between Fairly AI and anch.AI codebases are not established.
