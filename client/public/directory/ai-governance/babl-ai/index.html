Documented by provider

BABL AI's certification program consists of five core courses plus a capstone project and exit exam, aimed at auditors, AI governance specialists and risk professionals who perform AI risk and impact assessments.

Limit: It is a vendor-issued certificate; no external accreditation, ANSI/ISO 17024-style recognition, or regulator acceptance is evidenced.
