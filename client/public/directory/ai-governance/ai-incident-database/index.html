AI Incident Database (AIID)

Open, community-contributed catalogue of real-world AI harms and near-harms maintained by the Responsible AI Collaborative, searchable on the web and queryable through a public read-only GraphQL endpoint, with taxonomies for classification. Records are curated press-based reports, so coverage is uneven and not an authoritative incident register.

Documented by provider

The database states it indexes the collective history of harms or near harms realised in the real world by deployment of AI systems, invites public incident report submissions, and makes submissions indexed and discoverable.

Limit: Entries are derived from public reporting and community submission, so absence of an incident does not mean none occurred.

Source s1

Documented by provider

The repository states the application provides a read-only public GraphQL endpoint at /api/graphql, syncs content to an Algolia search index, and supports taxonomies for organising database content.

Limit: API stability, rate limits and data licensing terms are not stated on the pages reviewed.

Source s2
