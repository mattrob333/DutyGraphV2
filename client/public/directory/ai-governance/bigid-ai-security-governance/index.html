BigID AI Security & Governance

BigID's AI-oriented module inside its data security platform. It inventories AI models, agents, copilots, prompts, vector stores and pipelines, classifies the data feeding training, retrieval and inference, and maps lineage of that data. Claims come from vendor product pages; BigID's technical documentation is not publicly reachable.

commercial · generally available · Research snapshot 2026-09-06

Where it fits

Data governance & privacy · Agent discovery & inventory · AI risk & compliance management

Useful conversation with: CISO, Chief data officer, Privacy counsel.

Ask for a demonstration

Demonstrate how BigID builds an AI asset inventory that links a vector database to the sensitive datasets embedded in it and the identities allowed to query it.

Capabilities and evidence

Support labels reflect the supplied research. Documentation and vendor claims are not independent product tests. “Not established” means the researcher did not find support; it does not prove a capability is absent.

Vendor claim

Discovers and inventories AI models, agents, copilots, applications, datasets, prompts, pipelines, vector databases and shadow AI across the enterprise.

Limit: Product page; no documentation of scanners, supported platforms or detection method per asset type.

Vendor claim

Maps how sensitive data flows through AI training, tuning, inference, retrieval, prompts and downstream workflows.

Limit: Lineage granularity and whether it is derived from scanning or from platform metadata is not stated.

Source s1

Vendor claim

Provides scoped role-based access control by person, team or domain and the ability to track and restrict access to genAI data by user, model or agent.

Limit: Homepage marketing text; enforcement point for restricting agent access to data is not documented.

Source s2

Limitations to discuss
