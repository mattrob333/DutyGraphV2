Documented by provider

Dify is an open-source platform for building AI applications including agents, agentic workflows and chatbots that can draw on the user's own data and be published as web apps or integrated through a REST API, with a CLI for running apps from terminals, scripts, CI and AI agents.

Limit: Documentation reviewed does not describe permission models or approval steps.

Source s2

Documented by provider

Provides observability features with integrations for Opik, Langfuse and Arize Phoenix, and LLMOps monitoring and analysis of application logs and performance over time.

Limit: Application logs are described for monitoring, not as a tamper-evident audit log.

Source s1

Not established in this research

Human approval workflows, role-based access control, tenant isolation and audit logging are not established by the pages reviewed.

Limit: Absence of evidence; enterprise editions may document controls on pages not fetched.
